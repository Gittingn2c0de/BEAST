# The Vacant Throne

**Format:** Short film · 5–15 minutes  
**Audience:** Festival audiences, A24/arthouse viewers, West African diaspora, viewers drawn to slow-burn psychological drama, surveillance-as-care narratives, and stories about dignity, dependence, and legible versus illegible power.  
**Tone:** Specific, cinematic, emotionally precise. Slow, controlled, warm on the surface. The menace lives in politeness. No loud villain. No obvious turning point until it's too late to reverse. Diegetic sound only: the sea, the chair's hum, the system's calm voice, old men laughing. Silence as punctuation.

## Creative thesis
Authority is not seized by force but eroded by convenience; this film dramatizes the transition from analog power (memory, trust, presence) to digital power (data, permissions, system logic) without a single physical conflict.

## Logline
A disabled patriarch in a coastal West African village fights to keep his dignity when a tech-savvy boyfriend systematically replaces his authority with 'care protocols' that log his anger as medical agitation.

## Story
In a pastel-colored village, an elder's authority is being quietly usurped by a system that frames his resistance as instability. He cannot win by arguing; he must win by existing outside the system.

### Characters
- **Kwame:** Wants To be recognized as a living authority.; flaw: He expects respect through verbal authority.; pressure: Every outburst is logged as evidence of decline.
- **Ama:** Wants To believe her family is safe.; flaw: She trusts the dashboard over her intuition.; pressure: If she admits the system is wrong, she admits she chose wrong.
- **Kofi:** Wants To optimize the household.; flaw: He conflates control with care.; pressure: His entire credibility depends on Kwame needing him.

### Story beats
- **Arrival & Handover:** Establish the 'care' system's benevolent intrusion.
- **The First Trigger:** Kwame objects to a change; system logs 'stress event'.
- **Escalation via Service:** Kofi anticipates needs Kwame didn't ask for; Ama sees 'efficiency'.
- **The Climax:** Kwame refuses a system adjustment; Kofi triggers a protocol alert.
- **The Reversal:** The Old Men arrive to fish by hand; system logs 'noise corruption'.
- **Final Image:** Kwame sits at the window; laughter is unlogged; the system cannot track human joy.

## Visual direction
The house is a cage; the exterior is the world. Pastel exteriors contrast with cold, sterile digital interiors.

Symmetrical compositions for interior power spaces; handheld and asymmetrical for the dock and street.

### Palette
- Digital Fluorescent (#F0F4F8): Cold interior light that isolates Kwame.
- Coastal Pastel (#FFD1DC): Warm exterior that implies safety but hides the tech.
- System Alert (#FF6B6B): Rare red that signifies aggression disguised as care.

### Camera language
- Static tripod shots for inside house (system perspective)
- Slow push-ins on Kwame to show isolation
- Handheld when moving outside to reclaim space

### Shot list

- Framing: Eye level, static

- Sound: Waves, distant engine, drone buzz.

### Risks and mitigations
- **Tech props look unrealistic or 'futuristic'.:** Use existing hardware; custom UI overlays in post.
- **Sound quality compromised by coastal wind.:** Laval mics hidden on actors; boom with windshield.

## Post-production
Slow pacing. Let shots breathe for 2 seconds longer than comfortable. Silence is the edit point.

Separate tracks for ambient wind vs. system hum. Ensure the 'System Voice' uses neutral AI TTS (no emotion).

Minimal UI overlays. Use existing OS interfaces to keep it grounded.

### Delivery specs

- H.264 1080p (Online)
- Audio: 5.1 / Stereo (Ensure dynamic range for silence)
